Days 01–02 · Paro

Arrive · Chele La · quiet New Year

DayDatePlan
01 Wed 30 Dec Land Paro on the direct Delhi flight. Airport meet, permits, hotel. Afternoon only: a walk in Paro town, the exterior of Rinpung Dzong, Ta Dzong (National Museum) if energy is there. Early dinner. Acclimatise — Paro is ≈ 2,200 m; Chele La tomorrow is much higher.
02 Thu 31 Dec Day excursion to Chele La Pass (3,988 m) if the road is open — Himalayan views, a walk among prayer flags, and a quieter ridge beyond the standard stop if the pass is busy. If snow or ice closes it, we walk a Paro valley trail instead and keep the day outdoors. Evening: quiet New Year’s Eve in Paro — no gala unless you ask.

Chele La is the highest motorable pass in Bhutan. In late December it can ice over. We do not force the height on a 12-year-old if the wind or the road says no.

Days 04–05 · the warm valley

Dochula · Punakha · Khamsum

DayDatePlan
04 Sat 2 Jan Paro → Punakha via Dochula Pass (108 chortens, Himalayan range if the sky is clear). Short walk — weather-dependent, held loosely. If Dochula is crowded, the guide takes a quieter nearby viewpoint, then we drop to the valley. Punakha Dzong at a decent hour, suspension-bridge walk, check-in Dhensa.
05 Sun 3 Jan Rice-field and riverside hike to Khamsum Yulley Namgyal Chorten — a real walk, not a drive-up. Afternoon: optional gentle rafting on the Mo Chhu if the stretch is running in January (cold water; we only go if you still want it — extra, paid if you do). Evening: traditional farmhouse dinner with hot-stone bath — local house, not a hotel restaurant.

Punakha sits much lower than Paro and Phobjikha — a warmer night after Chele La and Taktsang. Farmhouse dinner and hot stone are in the package. Rafting is not.

Days 06–07 · the glacial valley

Phobjikha · cranes · nature trail

DayDatePlan
06 Mon 4 Jan Drive to the Phobjikha glacial valley — this is black-necked crane season. Check-in Dewachen or Gangtey Tent Resort. Evening on the Gangtey Nature Trail: open bowl, pine edge, prayer flags, no rush to a viewpoint car park.
