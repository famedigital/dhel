Before you travel

Essentials & etiquette

Dos & don’ts, documents, validity, and packing for your photography journey.

Dos

  • Dress modestly at dzongs, monasteries & temples
  • Walk clockwise around chortens and prayer wheels
  • Remove hats and shoes where requested indoors
  • Ask before photographing people or sacred interiors
  • Carry spare batteries · mountain weather drains faster
  • Follow your guide’s advice on altitude & pace
  • Respect local customs and quiet zones

Don’ts

  • Do not point with a single finger at sacred images
  • Avoid loud behaviour inside religious sites
  • Do not touch murals, statues, or ritual objects
  • No smoking in temples or restricted areas
  • Avoid revealing clothing on monastery visits
  • Do not litter — Bhutan values pristine landscapes
  • Do not drone without proper permits

Documents required

  • Valid passport (6+ months validity recommended)
  • Passport-size photographs (as advised)
  • Confirmed names for visa / permit processing
  • Travel insurance details (recommended)
  • Bagdogra arrival / departure timings
  • Onward Kochi tickets if connecting same day

Packing checklist

Clothing

  • Modest layers
  • Light rain layer (Aug)
  • Comfortable trousers
  • Sun hat / cap

Footwear

  • Walking shoes
  • Trekking shoes (Day 6)
  • Light sandals

Photo kit

  • Camera + spare cards
  • Power bank / charger
  • Lens cloth · rain cover
  • Reusable bottle
