Talking points you may use

Language

Short English explanations (they have Chinese text)

PlaceSay this, slowly
TachogangTemple and iron bridge linked to Thangtong Gyalpo, the bridge-builder saint. We walk slowly; the bridge moves.
SimtokhaOne of Bhutan’s early dzongs, 17th century, now a college. Today we photograph the outside only.
Buddha DordenmaGiant Buddha watching Thimphu valley. Good for wide photos. Inside rules if we enter.
Cheri / TangoCheri is a retreat monastery from Zhabdrung’s time. Tango is a shedra. Short walks. No photos inside if staff say no.
DochulaPass with 108 chortens for those who died in 2003. Wind is cold. Himalaya only if the sky is clear — not guaranteed.
TrongsaThe dzong that controlled east–west Bhutan. Kings were Penlop of Trongsa first. We take the long view.
JambayVery old temple. Tonight/today is fire blessing on the full-moon day. We watch from the edge. No flash. Jumping the fire is optional.
ThangbiVillage tshechu, not a national stadium festival. Mask dances. Stay at the edge of the courtyard.
TamshingPema Lingpa tradition. Famous old murals. We look a long time. Usually no interior photos.
KurjeyName means body-print. Guru Rinpoche’s meditation. Cypress and three temples.
GangteyNyingma monastery above the crane valley. Debate is a class, not a show. We ask permission.
Punakha DzongAt the meeting of father river and mother river. Winter capital historically. No photos in chapels.
KyichuOne of Paro’s oldest temples, same legend family as Jambay. Orange trees, quiet courtyard.
TaktsangGuru Rinpoche flew here on a tigress. The trail has cliffs. We can stop at the cafe viewpoint — that photo is already the classic.
