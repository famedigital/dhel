Kurjey · Jambay · Tamshing at a respectful pace. Morning meditation with your guide. Afternoon free for spa rest or a riverside sit. No packing the day — this is absorption, not ticking boxes.

Optional short valley walk or farm visit. Second hot-stone or massage if you wish. Pack lightly for tomorrow’s domestic flight. Early night — birthday eve if locked to 21 Oct.
