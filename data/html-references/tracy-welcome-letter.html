欢迎来到不丹

Dear Liu Zhong & Zhu Yiyong · 尊敬的刘忠与朱一勇

中文

欢迎你们。漫长旅途辛苦了——从今天起，你们可以慢慢放下肩上的紧张。 不丹欢迎你们；我们也把你们当作家里的客人。

我们的向导和司机不会说中文，你们也可以少说英文。没关系。 微笑、手势、手机翻译，加上耐心，就够让我们一路好好相处。 请放心：吃、住、车、行程，我们都已安排好。有任何需要，随时告诉我们——用中文发微信/WhatsApp 也可以，办公室会帮忙。

请慢慢走、慢慢拍、好好休息。这里是你们的旅程；我们只是陪在身边，让你们感到安心、自在。

English

A warm welcome to Bhutan. You have come a long way — from today, you can set the travel stress down. Please think of yourselves not only as guests of this country, but as guests in our care.

Our guide and driver do not speak Chinese, and you need not strain to speak English. That is perfectly all right. Between a smile, a gesture, a phone translator, and a little patience, we will understand one another well enough. Meals, hotels, the car, and your itinerary are already in place. If you need anything at all, simply tell us — a message in Chinese on WhatsApp is welcome; our office will help.

Take the days slowly. Photograph what moves you. Rest when you need to. This journey is yours; we are only here so you feel looked after — and at home.
