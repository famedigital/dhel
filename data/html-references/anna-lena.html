A note of welcome

One private nature journey · Kathmandu in and out · October–November 2026

Issue date · 19 September 2026

Dear Anna-Lena,

Thank you for writing — and greetings from Bhutan. Nehasha spoke of you after Tokyo; we are very happy you found us. This letter is the journey we have designed for you and your husband: ten days, simple and nature-first, from 26 October to 4 November 2026.

You asked for forests, wildlife, landscapes, hiking, and everyday Bhutan — not luxury. The circuit is one line: Paro → Thimphu → Phobjikha → Punakha → Haa → Paro, then Drukair back to Kathmandu. Botanical forest at Lamperi, the crane valley at Phobjikha, farm evenings in Punakha, the high woods of Chele La and Haa, and Tiger’s Nest on foot.

Your husband’s work as a biologist and forest scientist shaped the days. There is also something we can offer that no hotel list can. My father is a retired Bhutanese forester. He was among those who helped establish the Forest Division in its early years — including logging and the first forest administration — and he has watched Bhutan change from timber work to the country that keeps forest in its Constitution. If you would like, we will sit you with him in Thimphu for tea and an unhurried conversation. Please ask him anything. He is happiest when the questions are real. (This depends on his being well that week.)

One price, in three lines. Hotels are clean 3-star and farmstays, breakfast, lunch and dinner included. We ticket the Drukair flights. WhatsApp +975-17649720 · www.silverpinebhutan.com.

With warm regards from Bhutan,

Nights: Thimphu 2 · Phobjikha 2 · Punakha 2 · Haa 1 · Paro 2. Day 7 is a transfer day on purpose — hike Punakha on Day 6, not on the Haa drive. Black-necked cranes usually begin arriving in late October; this is the season, we never promise a sighting.

Why this order

  • Altitude eases in Thimphu before the high valley
  • Botanical Park sits on the road to Phobjikha
  • Phobjikha then drops naturally to Punakha
  • Haa & Chele La are the forest chapter — kept for later, when you are walking well
  • Tiger’s Nest last, when legs are ready

For a forest scientist

  • Lamperi mixed temperate forest & trails
  • Phobjikha wetland · RSPN crane habitat
  • Punakha subtropical contrast (rice, river, chir pine)
  • Haa / Chele La fir, hemlock, rhododendron
  • Conversation with a founding-generation Bhutanese forester
