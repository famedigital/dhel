Before you travel

Essentials & etiquette

Documents, packing for seniors, and temple manners.

Dos

  • Dress modestly at dzongs, monasteries & temples
  • Walk clockwise around chortens and prayer wheels
  • Remove hats and shoes where requested indoors
  • Ask before photographing people or sacred interiors
  • Carry any personal medicines & a simple first-aid kit
  • Tell the guide early if anyone needs a slower pace
  • Keep water and light snacks for long road days

Don’ts

  • Do not force long climbs if knees or breath are strained
  • Avoid loud behaviour inside religious sites
  • Do not touch murals, statues, or ritual objects
  • No smoking in temples or restricted areas
  • Avoid revealing clothing on monastery visits
  • Do not litter — Bhutan values pristine landscapes
  • Do not drone without proper permits

Documents required

  • Valid passport or Indian Voter ID / accepted ID for entry
  • Passport-size photographs (as advised)
  • Full group name list for permit processing
  • Travel insurance details (strongly recommended for seniors)
  • Bagdogra arrival / departure timings
  • Onward tickets Lucknow-side if same-day connection

Packing checklist

Clothing

  • Modest layers
  • Light jacket / shawl
  • Comfortable trousers
  • Sun hat / cap

Footwear

  • Soft walking shoes
  • Non-slip soles
  • Light slippers

Personal

  • Daily medicines
  • BP / sugar kit if used
  • Power bank
  • Reusable bottle
