Department of Tourism

SDF waiver · press / influencer

Based on DoT SOP “Press / Media / Influencer Trips to Bhutan” (updated 30 May 2023). Contact for questions: presstrip@tourism.gov.bt.

01 · What can be waived

SDF only

USD 100 / night SDF may be waived if DoT approves.
Your 7-night west circuit fits the standard waiver window.

02 · What is not waived

Visa · monuments · land

Visa fee and monument / entry fees are not waived.
Hotels, guide, SUV, meals remain a separate package cost.

How the joint application works

  • Silverpine (licensed operator) submits the Influencer / KOL form to DoT
  • DoT vets credibility, profile fit, and promised coverage
  • Target review time: within 7 working days of a complete submission
  • Approval is case-by-case — not automatic for any subscriber count
  • If approved, SDF waiver applies for the approved nights only
  • Operator must later forward published coverage to DoT

Nights · fit for your dates

  • Max 7 nights SDF waiver for Thimphu · Punakha · Paro only
  • Your plan is exactly 7 nights in those valleys — good fit
  • Up to 15 nights only if lesser-visited valleys are included (case-by-case)
  • Partners / friends / children are not eligible for waived SDF
  • Trips can run year-round — no blackout dates

Documents we will need from you

  • Media kit · channel analytics · audience geography
  • Passport bio page · travel dates · full itinerary
  • Written deliverables list (long-form, Reels, Shorts, tags, timelines)
  • Links: YouTube, Instagram, prior Bhutan / Asia travel films if any
  • Confirmation you will credit Bhutan tourism values & Silverpine as agreed

Sponsorship vs SDF (two decisions)

  • SDF waiver = government fee relief, if DoT says yes
  • Hosted / reduced package = Silverpine commercial choice
  • Waiver approval does not mean a free tour
  • We can quote: (A) full land · (B) reduced land if waiver approved · (C) hybrid
  • Filming permits (BICMA) may be separate if required for documentary shoots

Our position

We are willing to co-file your influencer SDF waiver with DoT once media kit and deliverables are in. Hosting level (full / partial / reduced) will be confirmed after we review reach and promised coverage — happy to do a short call.

Before you travel

Essentials & etiquette

Dos & don’ts, documents, validity, and packing for your documentary journey.

Dos

  • Dress modestly at dzongs, monasteries & temples
  • Walk clockwise around chortens and prayer wheels
  • Remove hats and shoes where requested indoors
  • Ask before photographing people or sacred interiors
  • Carry spare batteries · mountain weather drains faster
  • Follow your guide’s advice on altitude & pace
  • Respect quiet zones during monastery overnight

Don’ts

  • Do not point with a single finger at sacred images
  • Avoid loud behaviour inside religious sites
  • Do not touch murals, statues, or ritual objects
  • No smoking in temples or restricted areas
  • Avoid revealing clothing on monastery visits
  • Do not litter — Bhutan values pristine landscapes
  • Do not drone without proper permits

Documents required

  • Valid passport (6+ months validity recommended)
  • Passport-size photographs (as advised)
  • Media kit & deliverables for DoT waiver file
  • Travel insurance details (recommended)
  • Paro arrival / departure flight timings
  • Confirmed names for visa / permit processing

Packing checklist

Clothing

  • Modest layers
  • Warm mid-layer (Nov)
  • Comfortable trousers
  • Sun hat / cap

Footwear

  • Walking shoes
  • Trekking shoes (Days 3 & 7)
  • Light sandals

Content kit

  • Camera / phone + spare cards
  • Power bank / charger
  • Mic · lens cloth · rain cover
  • Reusable bottle
