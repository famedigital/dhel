What you do

  • Land Paro on the Delhi flight — the approach is the famous valley descent
  • Guide and driver meet you at arrivals · permits · bags · short briefing

Day 04 · Saturday 2 January 2027

Thimphu → Phobjikha

Focus
Long scenic drive · crane valley · first tent night
Hotel
Gangtey Tent Resort
Meals
Breakfast · packed / roadside lunch · dinner at Gangtey Tent
Expect
4–5 hrs driving · colder air near 3,000 m

What you do

  • Breakfast · check out · drive east toward Wangdue / Phobjikha (≈ 4–5 hrs with photo stops)
  • Road climbs and opens into the glacial bowl — black-necked crane wintering ground
  • Check-in Gangtey Tent Resort · we confirm underfloor heating is on before you settle
  • If light and energy remain: short stretch of the Gangtey Nature Trail — open meadow, pine edge, prayer flags; not a forced full loop
  • Evening: quiet tent, valley dark, early dinner — temperatures drop fast after sunset
  • Hot water bottles / extra blankets on request — tell the guide if anyone feels the cold

Expect a travel day with a big landscape payoff. Phobjikha is colder and often foggy or windy in January — dry bright midday, sharp after sunset. The tent is canvas outside, heated floor inside; it should feel snug if heating is live. Pack layers; ask for hot water bottles if anyone feels the cold. Tomorrow is the full valley day without a long drive.

Day 05 · Sunday 3 January 2027

Phobjikha · cranes · monastery

Focus
Full day in the glacial valley · no long drive
Hotel
Gangtey Tent Resort
Meals
Breakfast · picnic / lodge lunch · dinner
Expect
Cold clear air · slow walks · crane season

What you do

  • Leisurely breakfast · warm layers for the bowl
  • Gangtey Goemba (monastery) — visit respectfully; courtyard, views over the valley
  • Crane-watching from a quiet field edge or hide — January is black-necked crane season; binoculars help; silence matters
  • Continue or complete the Gangtey Nature Trail at your son’s pace — pine, meadow, flags; sit when you want
  • Optional: Black-Necked Crane Information Centre if open and useful for the family
  • Afternoon free at the tent · tea · rest · second night in the valley

This is why you sleep two nights here — not a drive-through. Expect frost on grass, bright midday light when clear, and quiet. Fog and cold are normal in January; we stay flexible — more walking if energy is high, more tea and viewing if the cold wins. No forced shopping circuit.

Expect the warmest, most “village Bhutan” day of the trip. Khamsum rewards slow walking. The farmhouse evening is included — smoky kitchen smells, local dishes, hot stone soak. Rafting is optional and often skipped in January cold; say no without guilt.

Day 09 · Thursday 7 January 2027

Tiger’s Nest · full trail

Focus
Paro Taktsang — the trip’s climax
Hotel
Le Méridien
Meals
Breakfast · café / packed on trail · dinner
Expect
4–5 hrs round trip · steep · early start

What you do

  • Early start from Le Méridien — typically leave ~7:30–8:00 (exact time set the night before with your guide)
  • Full trail to the monastery: forest path, cafeteria terrace (rest, tea, photos), then the steeper final stretch and cliff steps
  • We go beyond the café viewpoint to the monastery itself when the group can
  • Mules available partway for your son if he wants them (pay on the day) — still some walking remains
  • Time at the cliffs · quiet · descent at a safe pace — knees and dust matter more than speed
  • Return to Le Méridien · stretch · early dinner · bags ready; Day 10 airport pickup is timed to your confirmed Delhi flight

This is the day’s work — and why Paro is last. You are acclimatised after Thimphu ridges and Phobjikha. Expect dust, steps, prayer flags, and a 12-year-old who may need encouragement and snacks. Overnight Bumdra camp in January we do not recommend with a child this age. You fly tomorrow morning.
