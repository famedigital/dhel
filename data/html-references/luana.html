A personal reply · solo Bhutan · from our home to yours

Issue date · 8 September 2026

Dear Luana,

What a joy to hear from you in Brazil — thank you for trusting us with your first steps toward Bhutan. We would be truly happy to host you. This 10-day / 9-night private journey is shaped around the places that stay with travellers long after they leave: the capital’s quiet rhythm, Punakha’s river valleys, the sacred heartland of Bumthang, a scenic Drukair hop west, and the climb to Tiger’s Nest when you are ready.

You enter through Bagdogra (IXB). We meet you there, cross into Bhutan at Phuentsholing, and travel with care: Pling → Thimphu → Punakha → Bumthang → (flight) Paro → Tiger’s Nest → Pling → Bagdogra. For your flights into India we have outlined clear options from Georgia (Tbilisi) or Kazakhstan (Almaty) via Delhi, then on to Bagdogra — with booking links you can use yourself.

Along the way you will sleep close to Bhutanese life: in Punakha at Happiness Field Village Farmstay (www.hfvf.bt) or similar, and in Bumthang with an English-speaking family who welcome conversation as warmly as they welcome guests. In Thimphu you stay with us at Pelbu Suites. Pricing is kept clear — one package total, with the government SDF shown separately — so you can plan with confidence. We only ask that Day 7 (Bumthang → Paro) fall on a Tuesday or Thursday when the domestic flight normally operates.

Whenever you are ready, write to us with your preferred month and whether you fly from Tbilisi or Almaty. We will hold the dates and look after the rest. Silverpine Tours — Rajiv Pradhan & Tshering Lhamo since 2012. WhatsApp +975-17649720 · www.silverpinebhutan.com.

A proper capital day — time to understand why Thimphu feels different from every other Himalayan city.

Buddha Dordenma: construction began in 2006 and finished 25 September 2015 — about nine years — marking the Fourth King’s 60th birth anniversary. At roughly 51–54 m it is one of the world’s tallest sitting Buddhas; the halls hold tens of thousands of smaller gilded images.

Red rice fields, whitewashed temples, and a slower rhythm — Bhutan’s spiritual heartland.

Jambay is among Bhutan’s oldest temples (traditionally 7th century). Kurjey (“body imprint”) marks Guru Rinpoche’s 8th-century meditation site. Walk clockwise and dress modestly inside sacred courtyards.

The image that brought you to Bhutan — earned on foot, with pine forest and prayer flags.

Taktsang clings ≈ 900 m above the valley. Legend: Guru Rinpoche flew here on a tigress in the 8th century. The monastery was founded in 1692; rebuilt after the 1998 fire. Start early, carry water, go steady.

Before you travel

Essentials & next step

Solo traveller · Brazil · land entry via Bagdogra.

Dos

  • Dress modestly at temples & dzongs

Don’ts

  • Do not touch murals or ritual objects
  • No smoking on temple grounds
  • Do not book Day 7 off Tue / Thu
  • Do not cut Delhi connection buffers thin
  • No drones without permits
