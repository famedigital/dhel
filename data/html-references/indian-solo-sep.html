Your questions

Answered directly

The seven points from your enquiry · plus how we hold the trip for a solo woman.

1. Will the same guide stay with me throughout?

Yes. One Department of Tourism–certified guide is assigned to you from the Phuentsholing meeting through the Bagdogra drop. We do not rotate guides on a private tour of this length.

2. Will the guide travel with me in the private car?

Yes. Guide and driver travel with you in the same private car every day. You are not left to find taxis, and you are not on a shared coach.

3. What if I need to change the itinerary?

Day-to-day pace is flexible: skip a monument, add a café, rest at the hotel — no extra charge. Changing hotels, nights, or the route after rooms are paid may have a cost; we tell you before we move anything.

4. Any additional charges to budget for?

Lunch daily, drinks, shopping, laundry, spa / hot-stone, optional Tiger’s Nest pony, tips if you wish, and your flights or train to Bagdogra. Travel insurance is recommended and bought by you.

5. Cancellation / refund policy?

SDF / permit: not refundable once paid to the government. Hotels and services: 30+ days 20% · 15–29 days 50% · 7–14 days 75% · under 7 days 100%. Full wording is on the quote page.

6. Can you arrange a female certified guide?

Yes, if she is free on these dates. We request a female DoT-certified guide at booking and confirm in writing before you pay the advance. If none is free, you may wait, shift dates, or proceed with a male licensed guide. Tshering Lhamo is your woman WhatsApp contact either way.

7. When should I travel?

This quote is for 10 days / 9 nights — you choose the dates. Late September into October is usually clearer after the rains. We confirm hotels only after checking availability for the dates you send.

Safety · first solo trip

No night driving on mountain roads. Hotels with proper locks and private bathrooms. Your team stays reachable; Tshering is on WhatsApp. Bhutan is generally safe; the structure around you is what makes a first trip feel easy.

Pace promise

Tiger’s Nest slowly, with breaks. Free time most afternoons. Evenings for dinner — drinks if you wish. We never fill the day just because the car is hired.

How we walk it

  • Hotel breakfast · start when you are ready (not a race-group 6 a.m.)
  • Steady path · rest whenever you ask · guide stays with you
  • Mandatory stop at the cafeteria viewpoint — tea, photos, decide
  • Continue to the monastery only if you want to; turning around is not a failure

You are not an experienced trekker

This is a steep walk of several hours round-trip if you go all the way — not a technical climb. Wear shoes with grip. A pony to the cafeteria is optional and extra if you want it. After the hike: shower, rest, dinner. Nothing else on the clock.

The signature of the journey — taken at your speed, with a licensed guide who will not hurry you.
