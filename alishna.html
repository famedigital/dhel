Before you travel

Essentials & etiquette

Documents, dos & don’ts, packing, land & air validity.

Dos

  • Dress modestly at dzongs, monasteries & temples
  • Walk clockwise around chortens and prayer wheels
  • Remove hats and shoes where requested indoors
  • Ask before photographing people or sacred interiors
  • Carry spare layers for baby in the highlands
  • Share final flight PNRs for airport transfers
  • Follow your guide’s advice on pace & altitude

Don’ts

  • Do not point with a single finger at sacred images
  • Avoid loud behaviour inside religious sites
  • Do not touch murals, statues, or ritual objects
  • No smoking in temples or restricted areas
  • Avoid revealing clothing on monastery visits
  • Do not litter — Bhutan values pristine landscapes
  • Do not drone without proper permits

Documents required

  • Valid passport / accepted ID for all travellers
  • Baby documents as required for airline & entry
  • Confirmed GAU ↔ PBH tickets when issued
  • Names for permit processing
  • Travel insurance details (recommended)

Packing · family

Adults

  • Modest layers
  • Light rain shell
  • Walking shoes
  • Sun hat

Baby

  • Carrier + warm layer
  • Spare outfits
  • Nappies · bottles
  • Regular medicines

Useful

  • Power bank
  • Air-side snacks
  • Reusable bottles
  • Compact umbrella
